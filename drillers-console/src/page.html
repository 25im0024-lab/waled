    INSTRUCTOR / SETUP

    Scenarios (fault injection)
    Well / simulation

    Geohazards ON: limestone stringer 3140–3152 m, overpressure transition from 3152 m, gas sand 3215–3250 m (pore 1.30 sg, prognosis shows 1.24), depleted vugular limestone 3480–3495 m (losses). With MW 1.20 sg you will take a kick in the gas sand unless you weight up in time.

    Alarm set-points
    What is modelled

    String axial elasticity (WOB = k·(lowered length − drilled length), so drill-off tests work); ROP = f(WOB, RPM, bit hydraulics, overbalance, bit wear, formation drillability, founder point); top-drive speed loop with torque limit and stall; triplex pumps with liner size, efficiency and pop-off relief; Bingham/turbulent pipe and annular friction, bit nozzle ΔP, surface losses; mud transport as plug-flow parcels (kill mud reaches the bit after the string volume); BHP/ECD, cuttings load, swab/surge, pipe displacement and trip tank; influx from permeable zones and losses to weak/vugular zones; gas kick migration and expansion; BOP closing times and accumulator; choke flow with auto-choke; ISA-style alarm states.
