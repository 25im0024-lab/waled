What is modelled

The model includes the following:

  • Reservoir material balance with voidage replacement (water injection and gas reinjection).
  • Composite IPR: straight line above the bubble point, Vogel below it.
  • Wells solved by nodal analysis: tubing hydrostatic head with gas lightening and friction, plus the subsea choke.
  • Gas lift, and water-cut growth with cumulative production.
  • Manifold and riser pressure (hydrostatic + friction), a severe-slugging indicator, and arrival-temperature cooldown against a hydrate curve, with MEG.
  • 3-phase separator with level and pressure control, PCV/PSV to flare.
  • 2×50 % compressor trains (adiabatic multi-stage power); gas allocated to fuel, lift, export and reinjection; gas-turbine power balance with diesel fallback.
  • Hydrocyclone oil-in-water; crude BS&W.
  • Cargo storage and draft; the shuttle-tanker sequence with weather limits.
  • Turret weathervaning, mooring offset and line tensions; ESD/PSD.

This is a training model: the coefficients are illustrative and not from a specific FPSO.
